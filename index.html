Assessing, Modelling, and Managing Risk

This space is a curated digital portfolio documenting a 12-unit journey through security and risk management: covering qualitative and quantitative risk assessment, threat modelling, quantitative risk modelling, and disaster recovery design. Each unit records the tasks completed, a reflection on the learning, and the outcomes achieved.

Module Aims

The Security and Risk Management module aims to develop my understanding of how security risks can be identified, assessed and managed using qualitative and quantitative approaches. It will introduce methods for threat modelling and quantitative risk modelling while exploring how effective Disaster Recovery strategies can support organisational resilience.

Personal objectives for the module

As an IT Systems Engineer with practical experience in infrastructure, cybersecurity and Disaster Recovery, I want to use this module to strengthen the risk-management theory behind my professional practice. In particular, I want to improve my ability to quantify and communicate security risks, understand how threat-modelling techniques can support risk identification, and evaluate DR strategies from both technical and business perspectives. I also want to become more confident in using evidence and risk models to support security decisions rather than relying primarily on technical judgement and experience.
